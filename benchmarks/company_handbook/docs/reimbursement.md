# Reimbursement Policy

Employees may submit expense reports for approved business travel, client visits, and conference attendance.

## Deadline

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days after the trip ends. Reports submitted after the deadline require manager approval and may be rejected by Finance.

## Required Documents

Employees must attach receipts for lodging, meals, and transportation. Missing receipts should be explained in the report notes.

## Payment Timing

Approved reimbursements are usually paid in the next payroll cycle.
