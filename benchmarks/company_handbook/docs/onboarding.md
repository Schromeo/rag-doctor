# Employee Onboarding

New employees should review the handbook during their first week. The onboarding checklist includes payroll setup, security training, equipment requests, and team introductions.

## Expense Tools

Employees should submit expense reports as soon as possible and keep receipts for all business expenses. The onboarding checklist links to the Finance portal but does not define the reimbursement deadline.

## Manager Check-ins

Managers should schedule check-ins during the first 30, 60, and 90 days.
